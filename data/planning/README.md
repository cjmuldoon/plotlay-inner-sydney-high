# Approved floor areas (quoted)

Figures read from the NSW Planning Portal record for State Significant Development SSD 7610, Inner Sydney High
School: the approved plans stamped 28 February 2018, sheet 20 of 41, FJMT drawing 2801 "GFA Area Schedule" rev C.
They are quoted here as facts with their source, to check the floor space estimate. The documents themselves are not
in this repository: see https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/inner-sydney-high-school

GFA (gross floor area) is measured from the inside of the outer walls and leaves out stairs, lifts, plant rooms and
open terraces; covered outdoor terraces are counted as GFA in their own line. The floor space estimate in this
repository counts every storey over each footprint instead, so it is larger.
